I'm Phat and I am an AI Research Scientist at [Rizlum AI](https://rizlum.ai) in Paris, where I work on multimodal-model systems for document analysis, 
contribute to an AI training and Agent platform, and research and build speech-to-speech conversational AI systems achieving sub-1 second latency.

Prior to that, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in LLM at Toyota Belgium and LISSI, working on multimodal dataset analysis, multimodal LLM design, and an intelligent emotion agent, 
and an AI Research Scientist at [Trax Retail](https://traxretail.com), where I built deep learning models for image recognition and real-time on-device algorithms for iOS.

Before that, I did my PhD in Computer Vision under the supervision of [Julien Mille](https://julien-mille.gitlab.io/www/), Nicolas Ragot, Dominique Li and Donatello Conte 
in [Laboratory of Fundamental and Applied Computer Science of Tours](https://lifat.univ-tours.fr/). 
I received my engineer degree at INSA Centre Val de Loire.

During my universtity years (2015-2018), I have done my internships at the Laboratory of Signal and Image Processing (Rennes) and in the company of RF-Track. 
The domains that I have done during these two internships are mainly Embedded Software, Signal Processing and Machine Learning.
