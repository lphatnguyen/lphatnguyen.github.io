I'm Phat and I am an AI Research Scientist at [Rizlum AI](https://rizlum.ai) in Paris, with 7+ years of AI R&D experience, from academic research to industrial deployment, across computer vision, multimodal LLMs and real-time voice AI.
At Rizlum AI, I designed multimodal document-understanding models for insurers reaching over 95% correct extraction, developed our proprietary Text-to-Speech model with sub-200 ms latency, worked on Speech-to-Text (ASR) for our voice pipeline,
and deployed a real-time speech-to-speech system for conversational voice agents that automates 80% of membership sign-ups for our client MGP.

Prior to that, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in LLM at Toyota Motor Europe and LISSI, where I designed and trained multimodal LLMs (Mamba and Transformer architectures) and created an emotionally intelligent conversational agent, with related work published at IEEE ICIP 2025.
Before that, I was an AI Research Scientist at [Trax Retail](https://traxretail.com), where I built on-shelf product recognition models (over 95% accuracy across more than 500 SKUs) and shipped real-time, on-device algorithms in an iOS SDK (CoreML, ARKit).

Before that, I did my PhD in Computer Vision under the supervision of [Julien Mille](https://julien-mille.gitlab.io/www/), Nicolas Ragot, Dominique Li and Donatello Conte 
in [Laboratory of Fundamental and Applied Computer Science of Tours](https://lifat.univ-tours.fr/). 
I received my engineer degree at INSA Centre Val de Loire.

During my universtity years (2015-2018), I have done my internships at the Laboratory of Signal and Image Processing (Rennes) and in the company of RF-Track. 
The domains that I have done during these two internships are mainly Embedded Software, Signal Processing and Machine Learning.
